# Safety and freshness

Use this reference when the answer includes emergencies, health-related risks, weather, reservations, transport operations, prices, or entry rules.

## Confidence order

1. A current official source with an explicit verification date.
2. Repository data whose `last_verified` is within its accepted freshness window.
3. General preparation guidance clearly labeled as non-live.
4. `null` or an explicit unknown when no reliable fact is available.

Do not silently promote levels 2–4 into a current fact.

## Official tourism portals

- Use the city record's `tourism_portals` as a starting point for government tourism news, attraction notices, public services, events, and tourism-market alerts.
- Name the operator and show `last_verified`. A verified portal link does not make every page or repository fact current.
- If a legacy standalone bureau site is unavailable and the record supplies an active government fallback, describe the fallback accurately instead of presenting it as the bureau homepage.
- For current opening hours, ticket rules, closures, or events, prefer the attraction operator's current notice when it is more specific than the city portal.

## Emergency guidance

- Return the stored scope, source, and verification date with every number.
- Separate emergency services from non-emergency government or tourism assistance.
- Say that language support and local call handling can vary.
- For an active emergency, advise the traveler to move to safety and contact local emergency services. The Skill cannot place the call or guarantee a response.

## Health and environment

Altitude, heat, air quality, allergens, insects, and severe weather are risk prompts, not diagnoses. Recommend checking official alerts and seeking qualified medical help when appropriate; do not state that a traveler is medically fit for an activity.
