# Itinerary planning

Use this reference for multi-day or multi-city route requests.

## Inputs that materially affect the plan

- Origin and arrival gateway
- Dates or travel month
- Number of days
- Interests and must-see places
- Budget band
- Walking tolerance and accessibility needs
- Children or older travelers
- Dietary restrictions
- Preferred local transport

## Planning rules

- Keep Chinese and English place names together.
- Cluster stops by visitor area before balancing hours across days.
- Treat eight activity hours as a ceiling, not a target; lower it for slower pace, children, older travelers, altitude, or difficult terrain.
- Mark opening hours, ticket inventory, passport reservations, live routes, and transfer times as requiring verification unless a current provider was checked.
- Include a rain or closure alternative when the data supports one.
- End with a compact checklist of reservations and facts that still need live confirmation.

## Complete-plan handoff

For a full itinerary, include the recommended stay area, how to reach the first stop, transfers between stops, cultural context, nearby facilities that require checking, food guidance, seasonal packing, official weather/alert entry points, and the city's official tourism portal. Keep this supporting information concise for a focused attraction query.
