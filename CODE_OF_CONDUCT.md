# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community for everyone.

Be constructive, assume good intent while evaluating evidence carefully, respect cultural and linguistic differences, and avoid personal attacks. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, and deliberately misleading safety information are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when behavior threatens the community or the reliability of the project. Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer contact listed in the repository's Security or About section. Until that contact is configured, do not include sensitive personal details in a public issue.
